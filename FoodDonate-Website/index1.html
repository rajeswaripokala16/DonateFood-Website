How it works

📝

Post

Donors add surplus meals; seekers request what they need with location.

🤝

Match

We match by proximity and time. You get a private contact to coordinate.

📍

Pickup

Meet at a public, safe spot. Confirm freshness before accepting.

Safety & hygiene

  • Pack meals cleanly and label vegetarian/non-vegetarian.
  • Avoid sharing if food is spoiled, reheated multiple times, or past safe time.
  • Prefer public pickup points and daytime handoffs.
  • Wash hands and carry clean containers if possible.

Contact & help

For emergencies, contact local authorities or community kitchens.
